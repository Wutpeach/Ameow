Ameow 0.5.0 is a floating download app for Windows and Apple Silicon Macs. Copy a public media link, paste it into the app, and watch its progress as the file is saved. See [supported sites](./extension/supported-sites/) for scope.

![Ameow General settings: startup, theme and language](/Ameow/images/docs/rust-settings-en-general.png)

Choose your theme, interface language and startup preference in General settings.

## Start here

1. Choose and install your platform's version from [downloads](./downloads/).
2. Follow [Get started](./getting-started/) to launch the app, open settings, and choose a destination.
3. Paste a supported public single-video link, wait for the queue to show completion, and open the result.

To adjust quality, manage tools, or solve a problem, see [download components](./advanced/download-dependencies/) and the [FAQ](./faq/).
