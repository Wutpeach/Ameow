Ameow is a floating download app for Windows and Apple Silicon Macs. [Download and install](./downloads/), then launch from the Start menu or `AmeowLauncher.exe` on Windows, or open `Ameow.app` from Finder on Mac.

<ManualFigure kind="start" lang="en" />

Choose a destination in settings. Copy a public media link, move near the cat to expand Full, then paste. Versions with summon-shortcut support also offer `Ctrl+Shift+F` to open Full at the pointer. First use may need network access to prepare components. Once downloading and post-processing finish, double-click Full's empty background to open the destination.
