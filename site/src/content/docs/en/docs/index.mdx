Ameow is a floating download app for Windows and Apple Silicon Macs. [Download and install](./downloads/), then launch from the Start menu or `AmeowLauncher.exe` on Windows, or open `Ameow.app` from Finder on Mac.

<ManualFigure kind="start" lang="en" />

<Steps>
1. Choose a destination in settings and copy a public media link.
2. Move near the cat to expand Full, then press `Ctrl+V` or `⌘V` to paste. You can also press `Ctrl+Shift+F` to summon Full at the pointer.
3. Wait for the download and post-processing to finish, then double-click Full's empty background to open the destination.
</Steps>

On first use, Ameow may need network access to prepare download components.
