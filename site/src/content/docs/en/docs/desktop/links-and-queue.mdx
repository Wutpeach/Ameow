## Check and manage tasks

- **Downloading:** wait for post-processing and saving. A percentage may be unavailable when progress data is missing. Transfer shows network speed (such as KB/s); media processing shows a multiplier relative to playback duration (such as 2×).
- **Unfinished:** use Cancel and wait for stopping and cleanup to be confirmed before submitting again.
- **Complete:** open the task's submission folder. Clearing a record does not delete files.
- **Failed or partial:** read the message, copy diagnostics, or retry when offered.

The same unfinished video cannot be submitted twice. Tasks retain their submitted quality and folder; the queue is not retained after exit.

<details>
<summary>Retrying galleries and short links</summary>

- Image tasks created from Imgur image/album or Bluesky post entries retry in the same task, keeping verified files and attempting remaining content in the original work folder.
- Manual retries for videos and other generic entries create a new task with the original link, quality, and folder. Direct image links from these sites may also use the generic entry.
- An unidentified Bilibili short-link target cannot be retried. Submit the corresponding BV or av video link instead.

</details>

## Window and cat

The cat rests in Compact; download information appears in Full. Light theme shows a black cat, Dark a white cat. The cat stays still with the system's reduced-motion setting enabled.

While Ameow is running, press the default `Ctrl+Shift+F` to summon Full at the pointer. Press again while Full has keyboard focus to hide it; downloads continue. You can also restore a hidden window from the tray or Mac menu bar.

Right-click Full to choose [quality](../../supported-sites/#quality), open settings, hide, or quit. Double-click its empty background to open the [confirmed destination](../output-folder/). Quitting stops tasks and waits for safe cleanup; follow recovery prompts if needed. Collapsing the view or pressing Esc does not cancel quitting.

Change or clear the shortcut in [General settings](../settings/#shortcut). Summoning does not read the clipboard or submit a task. After expansion, moving the pointer away collapses or retains the window according to its current state.

For download or saving problems, see [Troubleshooting](../../troubleshooting/).
