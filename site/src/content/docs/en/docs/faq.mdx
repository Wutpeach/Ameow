## Where do I install it?

Choose the Windows x64 installer, portable version, or macOS Apple Silicon version on the [downloads page](../downloads/). You do not need Rust or Python to use the app.

## Which links work?

Public single videos and selected image/gallery links are supported; other public media links can also be attempted. Browser extensions and login-session import are not available. See [supported sites](../extension/supported-sites/) for scope.

## Where is the Mac Dock icon?

Ameow runs in the menu bar by default. Click the cat and choose open or settings. Mac requires macOS 13 or later; testing covers 26.3 arm64. See [first launch on macOS](../troubleshooting/macos-first-run/).

## Does hiding stop tasks?

No. Downloads continue while hidden; quitting stops tasks and waits for safe cleanup. See [floating window](../desktop/floating-window/).

## Does the cat show download progress?

The cat stays quietly in Compact; progress and the queue appear in Full. Light theme shows a black cat, Dark a white cat, and reduced motion keeps it still.

## When do quality and destination apply?

Both are captured at submission. Changing defaults affects new tasks; retry keeps the original settings. See [quality and formats](../advanced/quality-and-formats/) and [output folder](../desktop/output-folder/).

## How do app and component updates differ?

About updates Ameow and restarts the app after installation. Components management updates download tools. See [settings](../desktop/settings/) and [download components](../advanced/download-dependencies/).

## Can components work offline?

A healthy cache with signed proof can recover offline. A cache without proof needs online preparation. See [download components](../advanced/download-dependencies/).

## What if a download fails?

Check the link, task message, and original destination, then follow [download failures](../troubleshooting/download-failures/). Do not publish login details or private links in reports.
