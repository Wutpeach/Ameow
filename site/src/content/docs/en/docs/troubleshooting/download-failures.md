description: Download failures
---

Confirm a public single-video YouTube or Bilibili link and remove playlist or collection parameters. Content restricted by login, membership, or region may be unavailable; browser-session import is unsupported.

Check the task message and components management; restore connectivity and retry preparation as directed. Offline recovery needs a healthy cache with signed proof. Confirm the folder captured at submission still exists, is writable, and has space. Rate limits, network trouble, or site changes can also cause failure. Once conditions improve, manually retry a final failed task with its original quality and folder.

If failure persists, copy diagnostics and record shareable steps; do not submit cookies, tokens, or private links. There is no manual proxy setting; one HTTP error cannot establish its cause.

## Follow the symptom

- **Link rejected:** open the specific video, remove playlist or collection parameters, and confirm it is public.
- **Component preparation failed:** check components management. A healthy cache with signed proof can recover offline; an older cache without proof needs online bootstrap. Explicitly retry or confirm restoring the recommended version as prompted.
- **Save failed:** check the task's original folder, permissions, and space; submit a new task to use a different folder.
- **Site refusal or rate limit:** wait and retry, but do not infer an account or proxy cause from HTTP status alone.

If cleanup is unconfirmed, follow the app's waiting or cleanup retry prompt. Do not delete application data or replace cached executables to force recovery; failed-task diagnostics can help with a report.
