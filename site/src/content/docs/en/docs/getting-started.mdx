Choose and install your version from [downloads](../downloads/). On Windows, open it from the Start menu or `AmeowLauncher.exe`. On Mac, open `Ameow.app` from Finder.

The app starts with the cat in the compact window (Compact). Move the pointer near it to expand the full window (Full), where you paste links and see download status and the queue.

<ManualFigure kind="start" lang="en" />

## Your first download

1. Open settings from Full's right-click menu or the tray/Mac menu bar. Choose a destination in Download.
2. Copy a public media link in your browser, such as a single YouTube or Bilibili video.
3. Return to Ameow and press `Ctrl+V` on Windows or `Command+V` on Mac.
4. Wait for downloading and post-processing to finish, then open the saved file. Open the folder from settings or double-click Full's empty background.

On first use, the app may need network access to prepare download components. See [supported sites](../extension/supported-sites/) for public image and generic-link scope.

## Queue and destination

Select the count at Full's bottom-right to open the [queue](../desktop/links-and-queue/). Each task keeps its quality and destination at submission; later setting changes affect new tasks. Choose another folder in settings or drag one writable folder onto Full. See [output folder](../desktop/output-folder/).

## Hide and quit

Downloads continue while the window is hidden. Choose open from the Windows tray or Mac menu bar to restore it. Quitting stops tasks and waits for safe cleanup, normally without a dialog. Only cleanup problems display their cause and recovery actions. See [floating window](../desktop/floating-window/) for more controls.
