---
title: Supported sites
description: Supported public video, image, and gallery links.
---

Ameow 0.5.0 accepts public single YouTube and Bilibili videos, as well as explicit Imgur image/album and Bluesky single-post image links. Other public HTTP(S) links can be attempted, but are delivered only when the downloader can verify a single video or image and its result; success is not guaranteed. Site changes, regional restrictions, or permissions can still cause failures. Ameow 0.5.0 does not connect to the browser extension or import cookies or browser login sessions.

The generic entry does not imply support for arbitrary sites, mixed posts, large playlists, or web resource collection. See the [Electron v0.3.1 release](https://github.com/Wutpeach/Ameow/releases/tag/v0.3.1) and [versioned source](https://github.com/Wutpeach/Ameow/tree/v0.3.1) for the historical scope; sites may have changed since then.
