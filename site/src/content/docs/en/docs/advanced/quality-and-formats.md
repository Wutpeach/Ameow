Before submission choose Highest (no height cap), Balanced (up to 1080p), or Saver (up to 480p). Balanced is the default; actual formats depend on the site and video, and a cap does not guarantee that resolution. Accepted tasks retain quality, including on retry.

The old AE compatibility toggle is unavailable; verify the finished file in your target software.

## Choosing quality

| Choice | Height cap | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Highest | None | Prefer the best available quality; files may be larger. |
| Balanced | 1080p | Default for everyday downloads. |
| Saver | 480p | Prefer lower data and storage use. |

The result depends on media offered by the site. Exact resolution, container, and AE import compatibility are not guaranteed. Changing quality after queueing does not change the task; retry also retains the original quality.
